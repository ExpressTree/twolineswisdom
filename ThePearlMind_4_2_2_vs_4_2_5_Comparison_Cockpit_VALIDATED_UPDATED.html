Methodology & truth check

How this corrected cockpit should be interpreted.

Evidence discipline
Data truth

Validated with the clearer screenshots.

The current application replaces the earlier OCR-first version. Daily intraday strings and summary fields were rebuilt from the better screenshots you supplied.

Data truth

Common-date comparison is the fair comparison.

The clean apples-to-apples result is +63,333 vs +56,160 across 103 common dates, for a +7,173 advantage to 4.2.5.

Limitation

Full visible totals are not a strict A/B test.

The full 105-day and 106-day totals are useful operationally, but they include non-overlapping dates and therefore should not be used as the primary version-comparison verdict.

Limitation

Daily total can differ slightly from visible trade sums.

Individual trade values are displayed as whole points in the screenshots. As a result, Net Intraday + Carryforward can differ by a few points from the displayed Total Points on some rows.

Key insight

4.2.5’s edge is mostly intraday.

The same-date improvement splits into +6,188 from net intraday and +999 from carryforward.
