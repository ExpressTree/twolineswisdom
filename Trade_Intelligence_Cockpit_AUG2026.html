Simple meaning: Pick a better time + wait for a stronger signal + do not kill a good trade too early. August suggests that the best result did not come from trading everything. It came from being more selective and allowing strong moves enough time to develop.

✅ Remember this

  • Opening and after 2:30 PM were the strongest historical time zones.
  • 60%–69.9% confidence was the strongest confidence group with a meaningful sample.
  • If a good trade keeps working, give it room instead of rushing to exit.

❌ Be careful with this

  • 1:01–2:30 PM was the weakest August time window.
  • Below 40% confidence was historically weak.
  • Very short trades of 15 minutes or less mostly created churn rather than points.
