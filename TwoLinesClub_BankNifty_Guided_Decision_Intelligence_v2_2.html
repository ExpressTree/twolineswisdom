Step 1 · Start with the real problem

More trades do not automatically create better trading.

Many traders are not short of signals. They are short of a repeatable way to decide when to act, how much movement is still reasonable, and when a move is already mature.

Step 8 · Practice the decision, not the excitement

84 days is long enough to measure whether your behaviour is changing.

This is a practice and review window, not a profit promise. The purpose is to move from seeing → selecting → holding → systemising.

~1.5 minthis step
Days 1–14

SEE

Observe structure, distance and your own SL behaviour.

  • Record every trade
  • Mark early exits
  • Stop changing five things at once
Days 15–28

SELECT

Separate a visible signal from a trade-worthy candidate.

  • Review quality grade
  • Notice skipped impulses
  • Reduce unnecessary entries
Days 29–56

HOLD

Use travel, time and speed rather than fear.

  • Compare P50/P75
  • Track MFE and giveback
  • Study session pace
Days 57–84

SYSTEMISE

Turn useful observations into a repeatable routine.

  • Weekly review
  • Payoff ratio
  • Keep only rules that survive evidence
